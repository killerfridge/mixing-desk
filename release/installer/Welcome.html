Mixing Desk

Experimental beta for Apple Silicon and macOS 14.4 or later. Ad-hoc signed; not notarized by Apple.

Quit Mixing Desk before continuing. Closing its window leaves the menu-bar mixer active.

The app installs in Applications. The optional Mixing Desk Audio component creates virtual microphones and recording devices for other apps; it starts unchecked. Installing it requires administrator access and a restart. Close apps using virtual audio before changing the driver.

Sessions and presets are preserved.
